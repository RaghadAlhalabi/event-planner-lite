# Privacy Policy (Event Planner Lite)

**What we collect**
- Email address
- Display name
- Consent timestamps (ToS and Privacy)

**Why we collect it**
- To create and manage your account
- To show a public display name on events/RSVPs
- To document your consent

**Limitations & data minimization**
- We do not collect sensitive data (e.g., phone, address, birth date, payment info).
- We only collect what is needed to operate the service.

**Security**
- Data is stored only for operating the app.
- Access is limited to necessary application processes.

**Your rights**
- Account deletion can be requested at any time.
- Deleting an account removes personal data; public contributions may remain but are detached from personal data.

**Consent**
- Active consent is required before account creation.
- Consent can be withdrawn by deleting an account.
